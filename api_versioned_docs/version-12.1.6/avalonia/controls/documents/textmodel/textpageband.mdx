<div className="apiref-page__lead">

A page band at the text-model level: a nested [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument) with the
role and rule that place it and the identity a section's references name. The
owning document keeps its bands in [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.PageBands](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.PageBands), snapshots
them with itself, and restores them from a snapshot; the [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand)
element is the optional facade over one of these, the way [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument)
is over a text document.
