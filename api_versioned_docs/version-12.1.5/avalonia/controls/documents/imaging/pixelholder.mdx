## Remarks

The pixels are plain managed memory: immutable (never mutated after
construction), garbage-collected, and free of platform or UI affinity — so a
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Imaging.PixelHolder](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Imaging.PixelHolder) is safe to carry into a document snapshot and read
from a background serializer. Call [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Imaging.PixelHolder.Lock](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Imaging.PixelHolder.Lock) for a transient
[Avalonia.Platform.ILockedFramebuffer](xref:Avalonia.Platform.ILockedFramebuffer) view when handing the pixels to an Avalonia
bitmap or an image codec; the holder keeps owning the buffer.
