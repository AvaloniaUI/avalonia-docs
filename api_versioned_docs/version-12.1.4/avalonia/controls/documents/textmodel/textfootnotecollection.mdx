The footnotes a text document owns, in anchor order. Adding a note makes the
document its owner and joins the note's undo to the document's stack; removing it
releases both, and the membership itself is undoable when the owner records.
Ownership is exclusive, and a nested document (a page band, a note) owns no notes.
