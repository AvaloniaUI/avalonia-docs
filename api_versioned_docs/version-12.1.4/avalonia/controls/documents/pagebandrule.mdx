Which pages a band claims by itself. A band with [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBandRule.None](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBandRule.None) shows only
where a section references it; the other rules place a band from the document's
bands on the pages nothing explicit claims. The first band with a given role and
rule is the one the rule places.
