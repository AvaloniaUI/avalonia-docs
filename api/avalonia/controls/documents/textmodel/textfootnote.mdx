<div className="apiref-page__lead">

A footnote at the text-model level: a nested [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument) holding
the note's body, paired by [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextFootnote.NoteId](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextFootnote.NoteId) with the
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.RichFootnoteReference](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.RichFootnoteReference) anchor that carries the number. The owning
document keeps its notes in [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.Footnotes](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.Footnotes) in anchor order,
snapshots them with itself and restores them from a snapshot; the
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Footnote](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Footnote) element is the optional facade over one of these, the way
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument) is over a text document. The rendered number is the
note's position among the document's notes, never stored here.
