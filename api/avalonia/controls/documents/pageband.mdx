<div className="apiref-page__lead">

A page header or footer as an element: the facade over a [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextPageBand](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextPageBand)
the way [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument) is the facade over a [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument).
The text model owns the band ([Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.PageBands](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.TextModel.TextDocument.PageBands)); the owning
document's [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument.PageBands](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.FlowDocument.PageBands) materializes this element over it
on demand. Like any element, a band built from markup or code stays lazy: its
text document comes into being when the owner's document initializes or when the
band's content is first needed, and the model band when it joins the owner; until
then the owning document is its logical parent, as for a pending block. [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand.Role](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand.Role)
and [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand.Rule](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.PageBand.Rule) place the band by rule; a section's [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.Header](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.Header),
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.Footer](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.Footer), [Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.FirstPageHeader](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.FirstPageHeader) or
[Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.FirstPageFooter](xref:Avalonia.Controls.Documents.Section.FirstPageFooter) places it explicitly, whatever its rule, so one
band can serve any number of sections. It has its own buffer, selection and caret;
its undo joins the owner's stack; it never hosts footnotes or bands of its own.
